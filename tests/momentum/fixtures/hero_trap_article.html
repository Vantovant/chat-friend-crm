A teaching by Mr Masiya Baloyi · Teaching 1 of 7 in his series A Team That Duplicates

The busiest leader is often the one holding the team back

Mr Baloyi has watched it happen in teams, including his own. Someone gets stuck, the leader steps in and solves the problem, and it feels good to be thanked.

Every rescue teaches the team to wait. That is the hero trap. It starts as love, but it ends up becoming a ceiling.

It's a habit, and habits can change

The hero trap is not a character flaw. It is a habit, and every habit can be changed. The change is simple: instead of doing it for them, show them how, then let them do it.

This week's exercise

Write down three things you currently do for your team that they could do themselves without you. Then pick one and teach it this week.

Stop rescuing. Start teaching. Build people who build.
